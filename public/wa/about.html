About

Whisky Advocate is the magazine and website of record for whisky: ratings, news, features, guides, cocktails and travel, written and tasted by a dedicated editorial panel. It is published by M. Shanken Communications, the publisher of Wine Spectator and Cigar Aficionado.

How the ratings work

Every whisky is tasted by a member of the panel and scored on a 100-point scale. The score, the price, the ABV and the full tasting note are published together, and every rating since 2013 is searchable in the Whisky Finder.

  • 95 to 100 Classic. A great whisky.
  • 90 to 94 Outstanding. A whisky of superior character and style.
  • 85 to 89 Very good. A whisky with special qualities.
  • 80 to 84 Good. A solid, well made whisky.

The Top 20

Each year the panel names the twenty whiskies of the year from everything it rated. The lists back to 2017 are on the producer pages and in the stories.

FAQ

Are the tastings blind?
Bottles are poured for the panel without their labels. The taster sees the category and the stated strength, not the brand, until the score is in.
Where do the prices come from?
The suggested retail price at the time of the review, in US dollars. Shelf prices vary by state and by store, which is what the shelf scan is for.
How do I find a bottle I saw in a store?
Open the Whisky Finder and describe it, or use Shelf scan on the home page: photograph the shelf and the scores land over the bottles.
What does the cocktail cabinet do?
Tap the things you have at home, such as bourbon, lemon and bitters, and the cocktails page keeps only the recipes you can make with them. Garnishes are not counted against you.
Why does a bottle say "Price n/a"?
A few older reviews were published without a price. Those bottles are left out of value comparisons rather than treated as free.
How is "best value" decided?
By score against price within a category, so a 92 at $40 ranks ahead of a 94 at $300. The value map in the Finder shows the whole picture.
Can I turn the opening animation off?
It plays once per device. After that the home page opens on the news. There is a replay link under the ask bar if you want it back.
How do I subscribe?
Subscribe at the top of any page for the print magazine and the digital edition, or sign up for the newsletters in the footer.

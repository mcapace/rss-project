Retailer club

For stores that sell whisky and want to carry the magazine.

Stores in the Whisky Advocate retailer club receive copies of each issue for their shelves, a listing on the Where to buy page, and the Buying Guide ratings for the bottles they carry. Membership is free for licensed retailers in the United States.

What you get

  • Copies of every issue for the counter, with a display card.
  • A listing with your address, phone and website on Where to buy.
  • Shelf talkers for the whiskies the panel rated, with the score and the note.
  • The Top 20 each December, ready to display.

    The water

    Use the water you usually drink, bottled, filtered or from the tap; it is the taste you are used to. Skip water with a strong mineral taste.

    The temperature

    Room temperature lets the flavors through. Cold dulls sweetness and bitterness, which some drinkers like.

    The order
